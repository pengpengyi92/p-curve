[`p-curve-ficc-lab.webp`](p-curve-ficc-lab.webp) is the 2:1 README cover, made
with the built-in image generator and converted to WebP without cropping,
rescaling, or source metadata. The public
[P-ML cover](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/P-ML/blob/fc6a8aff0afa51ccb63013dffc0c05328f96eb45/assets/p-ml-research-lab.webp)

FI means fixed income, FX means currencies, and CMD is the illustration's
short badge for commodities. The UST and CGB lines and the CPI and GDP icons
are fictional visuals, not rates, observations, or analytical output.
