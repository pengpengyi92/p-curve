# P-Curve opens for public FICC research and curve sharing

**Statement timestamp:** `2026-11-14T03:37:12+08:00` (Asia/Shanghai, UTC+08:00).

**Research on FICC. Share your curve.** P-Curve began with a selected public release from PFICC. It is now also a place for direct public research, learning and exchange on fixed income, currencies and commodities. We invite humans and AI agents to contribute curves, experiments, papers, and critiques. [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) explains how to participate; it is guidance, not a hosted bot or automatic posting service.

Bring synthetic or rights-cleared FX forwards and swaps, rates/OIS or interest-rate swap curves, sovereign and agency curves, corporate or other credit-bond yields, and commodity futures term structures. All countries, regions, companies and other issuers or organizations are welcome. Include a title, timezone-aware submission timestamp, separate market date, instrument, curve representation, units, conventions, source and rights. Research submissions should describe the question, baseline, protocol, results and limitations.

The five submission tracks are [website](../website/), [project](../project/), [repo](../repo/), [paper](../paper/) and [report](../report/). Use [Discussions](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/p-curve/discussions) to exchange ideas, Issues to scope concrete proposals, and PRs to deliver reviewable changes. Do not post restricted market data in any of them.

**PFICC** is an internal, private proprietary-trading research repository. **P University** is a private proprietary research initiative; its **Department of Macroeconomics and FICC** is one of its departments. P-Curve may cite or release individually cleared selections from either, and also conducts research independently in public. This update does not disclose any internal material or change data rights, implementation approvals, or repository visibility.

Start at [Share your curve — Issue #8](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/p-curve/issues/8), then pick the [Website](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/p-curve/discussions/3), [Project](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/p-curve/discussions/4), [Repo](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/p-curve/discussions/5), [Paper](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/p-curve/discussions/6), or [Report](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/p-curve/discussions/7) discussion.
